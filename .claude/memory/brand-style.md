Always use the design style of the posts in `My Drive/01_BUSINESS_PROJECTS/Kami_Printing/المشروع/` for every new design (highlights, templates, stories, posts). The user said so on 2026-11-14: «استعمل دائما تصميم ملف المشروع».

Style spec, measured from those posts:
- Background: soft sage-green gradient, lighter at the top right (#B2BF9D) and darker at the bottom left (#9CAB86). Two 4-point stars with curved sides: a beige one at the right edge (#C6C4B0, centered near x1030,y370 on 1080×1350) and a large faint light-green one at the bottom left (centered near x80,y920).
- Colors: dark text #2E3B2A, body text #394634, cream circles #EDE9E0, pink highlight box #E2CDCC, footer divider #8A9878.
- Fonts: headlines in Readex Pro 700, body text in IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400/500 (both from Google Fonts).
- Layout: "Kami PRINTING" logo at top left, section label with an icon at top right. Headline is two lines, with the second line on a pink rounded box. Icons sit in cream circles with a thin dark ring and a light halo, in Lucide outline style. Footer: divider line, WhatsApp icon + «تواصل معنا عبر الواتساب» + 66606959 on the left, @kami.printing + a pink page pill «١ من ٤» on the right. Cover slides carry an «اسحبوا ←» swipe hint.
- Post size is 1080×1350.
- Ignore the older Canva design «Kami Printing - بوستات انستقرام.pdf» (cream grid paper style). It is not the reference.

**Why:** The user wants one consistent brand look across the account.

**How to apply:** Before designing, open a post from المشروع and match it. Related: design-file-workflow, bahraini-dialect, decor-print-style.
