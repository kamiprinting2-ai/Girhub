On 2026-11-17 the user shared reference gallery walls for the Kami shop's A4 decor prints and said «احب هذا الستايل». The style is retro or maximalist poster art:
- bold groovy display type
- vertical stripes, scalloped or wavy frames, checkerboard edges
- ticket stubs, playing cards, receipts and guest checks
- hearts and simple bold illustrations
- pink, red, deep green and cream palettes, in thin gold or oak frames

They rejected a calm, minimal set before this. A softer playful set was only a step toward what they wanted.

**Why:** They want the shop decor to feel fun, trendy and Instagram-worthy, not quiet or corporate.

**How to apply:** For wall art, frames or decor, use this retro poster language with Kami's colors, pushed a bit bolder (forest green, blush, rose, cream). Make original pieces, not copies of the references. Social posts keep the regular green المشروع style. Wall prints use logo colors only (D-012), no striped frame on the receipt print (D-013), printed matte 250–300gsm (D-014). Related: brand-style, bahraini-dialect.
