Write all text in every design in **Bahraini dialect** (اللهجة البحرينية). That covers headlines, body text, captions and calls to action. This rule applies to all designs from 2026-11-14 on.

**Why:** The brand is Kami Printing (كامي للطباعة, @kami.printing, WhatsApp 66606959), and its audience is in Bahrain. The user corrected words that weren't Bahraini: «سوا» became «معا», and «لمين» became «لمن».

**How to apply:**
- Don't use Levantine or Egyptian words such as سوا، لمين، هيك، شو بدك، كتير.
- Before finishing a design, read every line and swap any word that isn't Bahraini or plain Gulf.
- If you're not sure a word is Bahraini, use a clear neutral Gulf word, or ask the user.
- **Times of day are written in English with Western digits:** `8 AM – 2 PM`, `4 PM – 10 PM`. Don't use الصبح/الظهر/العصر/الليل or صباحاً/مساءً. The user asked for this on 2026-11-14. In RTL layouts, wrap the time range in an LTR bdi.
- In Readex Pro headlines, final ي after ف renders dotless («فى»), so avoid «في» in Readex headlines.
